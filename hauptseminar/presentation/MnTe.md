# Paper 1: Strain continuously rotates the Neel vector.
A. Liebman-Peráez et al., arXiv:2604.07653 [cond-mat.mtrl-sci] (2026)


## What is the Neel vector?
Primary order parameter
$$ \textbf{L} = \textbf{M}_1 - \textbf{M}_2$$

In $\alpha$-MnTe, the $Mn^{2}$ ions carry moments $S = 5/2, L=0$.

## Structure of MnTe
- two sublattices
- relation by six-fold screw rotation
- combination with time reversal
-> produces d-wave altermagnetism

- Also has a six-fold magnetocrystalline anisotropy (MCA)

## What is the reason for measurement

The authors investigated the main cause of the Neel vector rotation, the primary order parameter.
Thats important to know, becausewe need continuous materials with equal ordering for usable altermagnets.
Knowing that stress changes the order, means MnTe altermagnets need careful handeling.

## What Methods are used?
Magneto-optical measurements of MCD, $\phi_0$ and $\Delta$
- $MCD = \text{Im}[(r_{xy} - r_{yx})/2r_0] \propto L^3 \sin(3 \theta_L)$ with the Neel vector orientation $\theta_L$. ($r_0 = (r_{xx} + r_{yy})/2$)
- $\phi_0$ the birefringence angle
- $\Delta = \text{Re}[(r_{xx} - r_{yy})/2r_0]$ the birefringence amplitude
- Reference point: $\phi_0$ is the birefringence angle with respect to the \[100\] direction.

These measurements are closely related to the optical conductivity tensor $\sigma_{ij}(\omega)$, and the dielectric tensor $\epsilon_{ij}(\omega)$.

The measurements relate through the intensities measured by MOKE:
$$I_{\omega} \propto MSD$$
$$I_{2\omega}(\phi) \propto \Delta \cdot \cos2(\phi+\phi_0)$$

## What are the results?
While MCD and $\phi_0$ differ with the applied stress, $\Delta$ is unaffected. This means that the Neel vector rotates, but the magnitude of the order parameter does not change.
The measurements can be fitted to the $MCD \propto L^3 \sin(3 \theta_L)$ relation (normalized via $\Delta^{3/2} \propto L^3$), which confirms the rotation of the Neel vector.

## What are the conclusions?
- The Neel vector can be continuously rotated by applying stress, without changing the magnitude of the order parameter.
- The rotation is due to the competition between the six-fold MCA and the two-fold strain anisotropy.
- This has implications for the use of MnTe as an altermagnet, as the Neel vector orientation can be sensitive to external stress, which may affect the material's properties and performance in applications.
