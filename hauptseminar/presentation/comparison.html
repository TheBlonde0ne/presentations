Experimental Methodology Comparison

MOKKA vs. Mueller Matrix Polarimetry

Feature MOKKA (Dedicated MOKE) Mueller Matrix Polarimetry
Primary Output Pure Kerr Rotation ($\theta_K$) & Ellipticity ($\epsilon_K$) Full 16 elements ($M_{ij}$) describing complete optical transfer function
Acquisition Speed High Speed / Real-Time
Ideal for dynamic, rapid $M$ vs. $H$ loops via PEM/lock-in		 Slow / Static States
Requires multiple mechanical or synchronized optical modulations
Data Complexity Low post-processing; immediate physical parameter readout High; requires algebraic matrix decomposition (e.g., Lu-Chipman)
Anisotropic Crystals
(e.g., Altermagnets / RuO₂)		 Vulnerable to artifacts; mixes crystal birefringence with magnetic signal Flawless Separation
Mathematically isolates true magneto-optic cross-coupling
Depolarization Cannot decouple; registers as loss of signal intensity or baseline shift Explicitly Quantified
Isolates depolarization caused by surface roughness or domains
01
Core Trade-off: Choose MOKKA for clean, fast magnetic dynamics on isotropic substrates. Choose Mueller Matrix to decouple magnetic ordering from complex, anisotropic crystalline backgrounds.
