\section{Summary}

In conclusion the system specialized models can run stable simulations of a box containing 64 molecules for 1 nanoseconds.

The resulting self diffusion coefficients are a great improvement when compared with the "foundation model" MACE, with the dispersion corrected model only producing a deviation of less then 10\%.

Structural analysis show that the dispersion corrected model seems to structure the system stronger. Both models show an improvement of the first peak distance of the \(\text{O-H}\) bond RDF analysis, but overestimate the coordination number.

Improvements of the models could be made by increasing the training frame pool to cover more space on the Potential Energy Surface.
Further investigation of the importance of the neuron count in different hidden layers would be interesting.
