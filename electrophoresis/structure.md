## Overview of the system

We investigate the electrophoretic mobility and the diffusivity of short DNA segments (charged polyelectrolyte). We vary the length N of the segments and look for differences in the mobility and diffusivity.

Simulations include an LB fluid and explicit counterions (WCA spheres), and electrostatics handled by the P3M algorithm (a mesh-based FFT solver).
